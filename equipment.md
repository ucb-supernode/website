Electronics
===========

Electronics Bench
-----------------
![Lab bench photo]( {{ "/assets/lab_bench.jpg" | prepend: site.baseurl}}  )

Our general electronics benches are equipped with:

- [Agilent MSO-X 2014A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/MSOX2014A/mixed-signal-oscilloscope-100-mhz-4-analog-8-digital-channels.html) Mixed Signal Oscilloscope: 100 MHz, 4 Analog Plus 8 Digital Channels
- [Agilent 33522A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/33522A/function--arbitrary-waveform-generator-30-mhz.html) Function / Arbitrary Waveform Generator, 30 MHz
- [Agilent 34405A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/34405A/digital-multimeter-5-digit.html) Digital Multimeter, 5½ digit
- [Agilent E3631A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/E3631A/80w-triple-output-power-supply-6v-5a--25v-1a.html) 80W Triple Output Power Supply, 6V, 5A & ±25V, 1A
- Soldering iron and fume extractor

Surface Mount Bench
-------------------
![SMT bench photo]( {{ "/assets/smt-station.jpg" | prepend: site.baseurl}}  )

In addition to the general electronics equipment, the surface mount (SMT) bench
is equipped with:

- Hot air gun
- Board pre-heater and stand
- Stereo microscope

Power Electronics Bench
-----------------------
![power bench photo]( {{ "/assets/power-station.jpg" | prepend: site.baseurl}}  )

In addition to the general electronics equipment, the power electronics bench
station is equipped with:

- [BK Precision 8602](https://www.bkprecision.com/products/dc-electronic-loads/8602) 120V / 60A / 250W Programmable DC Electronic Load
- [Tektronix AM503B](https://www.tek.com/en/manual/am503b-am5030-ac-dc-current-probe-amplifiers) AC/DC Current Probe Amplifier
- High-power DC power supply

Fabrication
===========
![Fabrication table]( {{ "/assets/fabrication2025.jpg" | prepend: site.baseurl }} )

These tools can be difficult to use! Please be sure to attend a fabrication training and read the documentation before using. Feel free to reach out to a fabrication admin for help.

Bambu Labs X1 Carbon
--------------------
(high speed FDM 3D printer)

- 256x256x256mm build volume
- hardened steel 0.4mm nozzle
- textured PEI build plate
- fully enclosed
- AMS (the original) for up to four materials

- [quick-start bCourses module](https://bcourses.berkeley.edu/courses/1507589/pages/bambu-labs-x1c-3d-printer)
- [manufacturer's documentation](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/x1)

Prusa i3 MK3S+
-------------
(general purpose FDM 3D printer)

- 250x210x210mm build volume
- brass 0.4mm nozzle
- textured or smooth PEI build plate

- [quick-start bCourses module](https://bcourses.berkeley.edu/courses/1507589/pages/prusa-i3-mk3s+-printer)
- [quick-start guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1E7i_37JJ7uzfjOIiI92aGAVAVFuS9u4I/edit)
- [manufacturer's documentation](https://help.prusa3d.com/product/mk3s)

See also:

- [Beginner's Guide to 3D printing](https://drive.google.com/open?id=14Q1R5pcCLE6C0TUZaRwDbUTfdHCRZMy4B_Ujw6iPslA)
- [3 Tips to Become a Better 3D Printer](https://bcourses.berkeley.edu/courses/1507589/pages/3-tips-to-become-a-better-3d-printer)
