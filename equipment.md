Electronics
===========

Electronics Bench
-----------------
![Lab bench photo]( {{ "/assets/lab_bench.jpg" | prepend: site.baseurl}}  )

Our general electronics benches are equipped with:

- [Agilent MSO-X 2014A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/MSOX2014A/mixed-signal-oscilloscope-100-mhz-4-analog-8-digital-channels.html) Mixed Signal Oscilloscope: 100 MHz, 4 Analog Plus 8 Digital Channels
- [Agilent 33522A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/33522A/function--arbitrary-waveform-generator-30-mhz.html) Function / Arbitrary Waveform Generator, 30 MHz
- [Agilent 34405A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/34405A/digital-multimeter-5-digit.html) Digital Multimeter, 5½ digit
- [Agilent E3631A](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/product/E3631A/80w-triple-output-power-supply-6v-5a--25v-1a.html) 80W Triple Output Power Supply, 6V, 5A & ±25V, 1A
- Soldering iron and fume extractor

Surface Mount Bench
-------------------
![SMT bench photo]( {{ "/assets/smt-station.jpg" | prepend: site.baseurl}}  )

In addition to the general electronics equipment, the surface mount (SMT) bench
is equipped with:

- Hot air gun
- Board pre-heater and stand
- Stereo microscope

Power Electronics Bench
-----------------------
![power bench photo]( {{ "/assets/power-station.jpg" | prepend: site.baseurl}}  )

In addition to the general electronics equipment, the power electronics bench
station is equipped with:

- [BK Precision 8602](https://www.bkprecision.com/products/dc-electronic-loads/8601-250-w-programmable-dc-electronic-load.html) 120V / 60A / 250W Programmable DC Electronic Load
- [Tektronix AM503B](https://www.tek.com/en/manual/am503b-am5030-ac-dc-current-probe-amplifiers) AC/DC Current Probe Amplifier
- High-power DC power supply
